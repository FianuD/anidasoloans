Who We Are

More than a loan app.
A financial partner you can trust.

Anidaso means "Hope" in Twi. We founded Anidaso Loans to bridge the financial gap for everyday Ghanaians without compromising their privacy or peace of mind. By leveraging secure Mobile Money (MoMo) technology and strictly adhering to the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), we provide instant liquidity with zero predatory practices.

Whether you are a small business owner needing working capital, or a family handling an emergency, our mission is to move you forward safely and simply.

The Policy Shift

Before vs. After

A side-by-side contrast of the predatory data practices we reject versus the Act 843–enforced governance we now ship.

The Past · Competitors

Predatory Practices

  • ✕

    Illegal Contact Harvesting

    Rogue apps request full phone-book access, silently exfiltrating names, numbers, and relationships of every contact.

  • ✕

    SMS Log Reading

    Apps parse incoming and outgoing SMS to build shadow credit profiles, reading OTPs, transaction alerts, and personal messages.

  • ✕

    Social-Shaming Debt Tactics

    Default triggers mass notifications to every harvested contact. Debt collection becomes public humiliation and family disruption.

  • ✕

    Indefinite Data Retention

    Personal data is retained forever, far beyond the loan lifecycle, with no Right to Erasure and no DPO oversight.

The Anidaso Standard

Act 843 Enforced Governance

  • ✓

    Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

    Loan officers see credit data only. Support agents see account status only. No single role can access the full data graph.

  • ✓

    Automated Data Minimization at Sign-Up

    The system requests only MoMo identity, national ID, and income declaration. No contact access. No SMS read. No social-graph traversal.

  • ✓

    Zero Boundary Violations

    Emergency contacts are optional, time-limited (72-hour activation window), and explicitly scoped. No skip-tracing. No social shaming. Period.

  • ✓

    Lifecycle-Scoped Retention

    365-day hard purge after loan settlement. Full Right to Erasure honored. DPO audits every processing decision.

Our Commitment

Three Pillars of Consumer Dignity

Borrowers are not data points. They are people with rights, privacy, and the dignity to control their own information.

Pillar 01

Consent Control

Borrowers can explicitly grant or revoke alternate data access dynamically through a real-time consent dashboard. No pre-checked boxes. No bundled permissions. Every data point is an individual, revocable decision.

Act 843 § 20 — Explicit Consent

Pillar 02

Dignified Recovery

Strict operational limits on skip-tracing scripts. No contact harassment. Debt collection is completely decoupled from private personal relationships. Recovery is a financial conversation, never a social one.

Bank of Ghana’s Directive for Digital Credit Services Providers, 2025. § 7 — Fair Treatment

Pillar 03

Lifecycle Deletion

Automatic archiving and purging of data records once loans are settled, fully respecting the Right to Erasure under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). 365-day hard purge. No indefinite retention.

Act 843 § 24 — Right to Erasure
